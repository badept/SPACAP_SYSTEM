Prompt specification: Create one landscape 3:2 sprite atlas with an exact 4-column by 2-row grid, equal cells, a white background, generous padding and no text or grid borders. Use consistent glossy blue, cyan and white isometric miniatures. Row 1: database with charts; mobile phone with map pin, store and checklist; megaphone with gift and percent sign; retail display shelf with checklist. Row 2: trophy with star and display photograph; laptop with event, check-in and chart; merchandising shop with shirts and bottles; dark blue globe with cyan grid, glowing dots and pedestal. Keep each complete illustration inside its own cell, separated from neighboring subjects.

CSS displays individual cells without modifying the generated bitmap. The eight cells correspond to Sampling, Selling Activation, Promotion / Đổi quà, Trưng bày, Cuộc thi trưng bày, Roadshow / Event, Merchandising, and the sidebar globe.
